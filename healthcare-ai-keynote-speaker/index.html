Speaking

Healthcare AI keynote speaker

I'm Jan Beger, Global Head of AI Advocacy at GE HealthCare and Executive Director of HelloAI. I give keynotes, panels, and workshops on AI in healthcare. Most speakers on this topic talk to the people building the technology. I talk to the people who have to make it work: clinicians, radiologists, and the executives who answer for the results.

Every day I summarize a healthcare AI research paper for 92,000 people on LinkedIn, and once a month I send the ten that mattered to 45,000 newsletter subscribers. On Substack I write Second Opinion for health system leaders. HelloAI, a program I lead, has trained more than 10,000 healthcare professionals from over 100 countries since 2018. That work keeps me current on what is actually landing in hospitals versus what is being oversold. A talk from me is not a tour of the latest models. It is an honest account of what works at the bedside, what fails, and what a room full of clinicians or executives should do on Monday.

One talk, tailored to your audience

I give one talk and adapt its content and title to each event. The core stays the same: the hard part of healthcare AI isn't the algorithm. It's trust, workflow, and the question of who owns the decision when the AI is wrong. Depending on the audience, I put the weight on:

  • Radiology and medical imaging

    What changes and what doesn't for diagnostic radiology, drawn from 20+ years in medical imaging IT and a daily read of the literature. More for radiology audiences.

  • From pilots to practice

    How health systems move AI out of the pilot stage and into routine use, and why most of the value is in how people work, not the tool.

  • AI fluency in healthcare

    Why awareness training doesn't produce competent users, and what fluency actually takes. More on AI fluency.

  • When AI does the work, humans can care

    My TEDx talk from 2026: the future of healthcare isn't algorithms alone, it's how AI and human care work together. Watch the talk.

Beyond healthcare

I also speak at leadership, corporate, and cross-industry events. Healthcare is AI’s hardest test: lives are at stake, regulation is strict, and the people using it rightly ask who answers for a wrong decision. What holds up there holds up in any organization bringing AI into its work.

  • Trust and accountability

    When people should rely on an AI result, when they should overrule it, and who owns the decision either way.

  • Adoption is a people problem

    Why most AI projects stall between pilot and practice, and what changes when you design for the people doing the work.

  • AI fluency for a whole workforce

    Why awareness training doesn’t make people competent users, and what builds real skill.

  • People and AI at work

    Which tasks AI should take over, and how roles change when it does.

My audiences already reach well beyond healthcare, from Hogan Lovells and Roche to Telefónica’s Innovation Day, the Big Bang KI Festival in Berlin, and the AI Action Summit in Paris. KPMG and Porsche Consulting have featured my work.
