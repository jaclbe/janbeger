Bio

Jan Beger is Global Head of AI Advocacy at GE HealthCare and Executive Director of HelloAI, where he helps healthcare professionals turn AI from theory into practical clinical impact. With more than 20 years of experience in healthcare informatics, medical imaging, and AI, he focuses on the real challenge of healthcare AI: not the algorithms, but trust, workflow, adoption, and patient benefit. Since 2018, HelloAI has trained more than 10,000 healthcare professionals from over 100 countries.

Jan is known for translating complex AI developments into clear, actionable insights for clinicians and health system leaders. Every day he summarizes a new AI research paper on LinkedIn for more than 92,000 healthcare professionals, and each month his LinkedIn newsletter, Interesting Reads, brings the ten papers that mattered to more than 45,000 subscribers.

He also founded Edison Accelerator, a collaboration platform connecting providers, startups, and industry leaders to accelerate AI adoption in healthcare, and GR4AI, a non-profit book project that helps children aged seven to twelve understand artificial intelligence and how it shapes the world around them.

On stage, Jan speaks about how AI can support clinicians, improve workflows, and make healthcare more human while helping organizations move from AI hype to real-world implementation.
